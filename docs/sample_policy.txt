Acme Company Leave Policy

Annual leave: Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused annual leave up to 10 days can be carried over to the next year.

Sick leave: Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required if sick leave is longer than 3 consecutive days.

Work from home: Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Requests must be submitted at least one day in advance through the HR portal.

Notice period: The standard notice period for resignation is 60 days.
